# Sources and query bank - wellness-news

6 lanes. Run 2-4 queries per lane per week. Replace {month} / {year} with the current month and year.

## Lane 1 - Wearables, health tech, recovery tech (Shai's strongest lane)
**Sources:** Bloomberg (Mark Gurman - Power On, Sundays), The Verge, 9to5Mac, MacRumors, Wareable, DC Rainmaker, TechCrunch, company newsrooms (Apple, Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, Samsung, Google/Fitbit, Hyperice, Therabody)
**Queries:**
- `Apple health wearable report {month} {year}`
- `Oura OR WHOOP new feature announcement`
- `recovery technology launch {month} {year}`
- `Mark Gurman Apple Watch health`

## Lane 2 - Wellness business, deals and trends
**Sources:** Athletech News, Club Industry, Global Wellness Institute, Global Wellness Summit (trend reports), ClassPass / Mindbody reports, Business of Fashion, CNBC, Reuters, Axios
**Queries:**
- `wellness industry acquisition OR funding {month} {year}`
- `Global Wellness Institute report {year}`
- `ClassPass OR Mindbody trend report {year}`
- `Nike OR Lululemon OR Hyrox wellness news`

## Lane 3 - Workplace wellbeing and burnout (Yoav's core - LinkedIn)
**Sources:** Gallup (State of the Global Workplace, Workplace blog), SHRM, HR Dive, Business Group on Health, Mercer, Aon, WTW, Deloitte, McKinsey Health Institute, WHO, Harvard Business Review, Fortune (Work), Oxford Wellbeing Research Centre
**Queries:**
- `employee burnout study {month} {year}`
- `Gallup workplace wellbeing {year}`
- `manager burnout survey {year}`
- `employer wellbeing benefits trend {year}`
- `return to office wellbeing survey`

## Lane 4 - Longevity, cold, breathing, recovery science
**Sources:** Nature / JAMA / BMJ news, ScienceDaily, Stanford Medicine, Huberman Lab / Peter Attia (as leads only, verify the underlying study), New York Times Well, Washington Post Wellness
**Queries:**
- `cold water immersion study {year}`
- `breathwork stress study {year}`
- `sauna OR cold plunge research new`
- `longevity healthspan study {month} {year}`
- `sleep recovery study workers`

## Lane 5 - Wellness travel, hospitality, real estate, luxury
**Sources:** Skift, Travel + Leisure, Condé Nast Traveler, Hilton / Marriott / Aman / Six Senses newsrooms, Global Wellness Institute (real estate), Gulf news (UAE, Saudi)
**Queries:**
- `wellness retreat launch luxury hotel {year}`
- `wellness tourism report {year}`
- `longevity resort OR clinic opening {year}`
- `wellness real estate office building`

## Source credibility lines (use in posts)
- Bloomberg / Gurman: "הכתב הבכיר של בלומברג לענייני אפל. יש לו משקל, אבל זו עוד לא הכרזה רשמית."
- Gallup: "סקר על יותר מ-100 אלף עובדים ב-140 מדינות" (check the exact N each time)
- A company press release: "לפי ההודעה הרשמית של ___"
- A single small study: "מחקר אחד, קטן יחסית. מעניין, לא מוכח."
